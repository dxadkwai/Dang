FAQs

What exactly does LeftClick do?+

LeftClick is an AI growth partner for B2B companies. We build end-to-end AI systems that generate leads, close deals, and scale operations — covering everything from outbound automation and CRM workflows to custom web apps, hiring systems, and backend AI infrastructure.

How quickly can we start seeing results?+

Most clients see their first working system deployed within 2–4 weeks of kickoff. We move fast because we build, test, and deploy — we don't spend months on strategy decks. The free Funnel Map Audit gives you a clear picture of timelines before you commit to anything.

How does pricing work?+

We charge a fixed project price — no hourly billing, no surprise invoices. After the free Funnel Map Audit, we deliver a clear proposal with a defined scope, timeline, and price. Optional ongoing retainers are available for monitoring and optimization after launch.

Do you work with any type of B2B company?+

We work best with fast-moving B2B companies — SaaS, agencies, professional services, and tech-enabled businesses — that are already generating revenue and want to scale intelligently with AI. We've worked with companies from seed stage to enterprise, including Anthropic, Notion, Wix, HeyGen, and VEED.

What makes LeftClick different from a regular agency?+

We're AI-native, not AI-curious. Nick was using GPT-2 and GPT-3 to build real businesses in 2020 — well before ChatGPT existed. Our team has shipped production-grade systems for 200+ companies, including governments and Fortune 500s. We fix real, practical problems — not theoretical ones that look good on paper.

Can you handle both strategy and technical implementation?+

Yes — that's what makes us different. Most consultants hand you a roadmap and disappear. Most dev shops build what you tell them without strategy. We do both: we figure out the right problems to solve, then we actually build and deploy the solutions ourselves.

How do I get started?+

Book a free 30-minute Funnel Map Audit. We'll review your current systems, identify your biggest growth bottlenecks, and walk you through exactly how AI can move the needle for your specific business. No pitch, no pressure — just a clear look at the opportunity.

Do you offer any training or educational resources?+

Yes. Nick runs one of the largest AI channels on YouTube and manages Maker School — the #1 no-code community on Skool. We also offer team AI workshops and roadmapping sessions for companies that want to upskill internally alongside a full engagement.
